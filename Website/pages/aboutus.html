About

About the academy

We teach the practical side of looking after pets: training, grooming, feeding, socialising and first aid.

How it started

Sarah Smith started Pawsitive Pet Academy in Durban in 2023. She noticed that lots of people love their pets but aren't sure how to feed them properly, train them or help them in an emergency. Since then, hundreds of pet owners and future pet care workers have finished our six-week and six-month courses.

Our vision

To be the first place people in Durban think of when they want to learn about pet care and training.

Our mission

To give pet owners and pet care workers hands-on skills they can use straight away, so their animals stay happy and healthy.

Our goals

  • Help more owners look after their pets properly.
  • Train people for jobs in the pet care industry.
  • Help people start their own pet businesses.
