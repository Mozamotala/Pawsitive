About Us

Who we are.

Pawsitive Pet Academy teaches practical pet care and training, including grooming, feeding, socialisation and emergency care.

History

Pawsitive Pet Academy was started in 2023 by Sarah Smith in Durban. She saw that many people own pets but do not know much about pet care, training, feeding or emergency care. Since then, hundreds of pet owners and people wanting to work with pets have finished our six-month and six-week courses.

Vision

To be the go-to place in Durban for learning how to care for and train pets.

Mission

To give pet owners and pet care workers practical, hands-on skills that keep pets happy and healthy.

Goals

  • Help more pet owners look after their pets properly.
  • Train people for jobs in the pet care industry.
  • Help people start their own pet businesses.
